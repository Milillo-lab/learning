Interactive modules from the RiSE Lab for hands-on radar remote sensing. Each one pairs the underlying theory with something you can steer yourself — on real satellite data where possible.

Modules

SAR

SAR Geometry

Look angle, incidence, slant vs ground range, foreshortening, layover and shadow, and ellipsoid geocoding — four exercises, two on real Umbra scenes and two on the equations.

MODE

SAR Acquisition Modes

A 3D animated explainer of stripmap, spotlight, ScanSAR, TOPS and SweepSAR — orbit the scene, watch the beam move over the ground, and see where each mode's coverage and duty cycle come from.

CIVE 6359 · 5 modes · interactive 3D
InSAR

Height of Ambiguity

A fringe simulator on real terrain: change the baseline, the band and the incidence angle and watch topographic fringes on Fuego respond, then see how an out-of-date DEM leaves fringes at Kirishima.
