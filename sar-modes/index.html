SAR Acquisition Modes

A radar builds an image while it flies. What it points its beam at as it goes — held fixed, steered onto one spot, hopped between swaths, or swept on receive — is the difference between the acquisition modes, and it sets the trade between resolution and coverage. Pick a mode and watch the beam.
